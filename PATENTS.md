# Patent Pledge

*In the spirit of Tesla's 2014 pledge, ["All Our Patent Are Belong To
You."](https://www.tesla.com/blog/all-our-patent-are-belong-you)*

Pocket Connect is released under the **Apache License 2.0**. Section 3 of that
license already grants every user a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free patent
license to any patent claims we hold that are necessarily infringed by this
software. Read that first — it is the binding legal grant.

Beyond that grant, and in plain language, we pledge:

> **We will not initiate patent litigation against anyone for making, using,
> selling, offering to sell, importing, or distributing Pocket — or any
> technology it embodies — so long as they are acting in good faith.**

You are **acting in good faith** for as long as you:

1. **do not** assert, or voluntarily help a third party assert, any patent or
   other intellectual-property claim against Pocket, its maintainers, its
   contributors, or its users, for their use of Pocket;
2. **do not** challenge, or voluntarily help a third party challenge, the
   validity or enforceability of any patent that covers this project; and
3. **do not** sell or market a direct clone of Pocket while passing it off as
   made, endorsed, or affiliated with us (see the trademark note below).

If you take any of those actions, this pledge no longer applies to you — the same
defensive-termination principle already built into Apache 2.0 §3. Fork it, ship
it, build a business on it: that is the point. Weaponize patents against the
commons: the pledge is void for you.

## Not covered by this pledge

- **Trademarks.** The name *Pocket*, the wordmark, the pocket icon, and the
  brand identity (see `pocket-brand` CIS) are **not** open source. Apache 2.0 §6
  grants no trademark rights. Fork the code freely; sew your own name and mark on
  it. Don't ship a look-alike under ours.
- **Third-party components.** Dependencies keep their own licenses.

technology. It does not grant rights beyond the Apache 2.0 `LICENSE`, does not
create a contract, and is not legal advice. Where this pledge and the `LICENSE`
